Featured Writer: D. Bryan Knight

Discover the lyrical genius behind the sunset melodies.

"So many technological advances have taken place in the music business since '88 but one thing has never changed in my mind and that is that there will always be room for one more great and beautifully written song that will change your perspective, if only for a moment, and that's what keeps me dreaming!

What you will find in this catalog of music are songs that have been written, re-written, recorded and re-recorded to get them to a level that will grab the listener.

I truly, hope you enjoy what you hear. Not just from my songs but of the other talented writers who have lived their music and strive for that one, great song! All of our songs are fully copyrighted, and all IPI numbers are assigned accordingly.

ENJOY!"

— D. Bryan Knight
